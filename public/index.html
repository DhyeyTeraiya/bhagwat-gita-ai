॥ श्री कृष्ण उवाच ॥

परित्राणाय साधूनां विनाशाय च दुष्कृताम्।
धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय सम्भवामि युगे युगे॥

Welcome to Vrindavan - Seek Divine Guidance from Lord Krishna

My dear devotee,

Welcome to Vrindavan, my divine abode. As the gentle breeze carries the melody of my flute, let your heart be at peace. Share your thoughts, concerns, or seek guidance about your life's path. Just as I guided Arjuna in the battlefield of Kurukshetra, I am here to illuminate your path with the eternal wisdom of the Bhagavad Gita.

What guidance do you seek today?

~ श्री कृष्ण
